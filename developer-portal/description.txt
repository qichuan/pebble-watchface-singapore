Crescent Field is the Singapore flag, reduced to a chord.

The red band is cut by the display edge, so the crescent and its five stars sit
exactly where they do on the flag — top-left, small, unfussy. Everything below
the cut is white space for the hour.

On a round watch the cut lands on the diameter, the longest chord the glass has
to offer. The shape the flag makes on a circle is not a compromise here; it is
the reason the face exists.

The five stars are five-pointed, as they are on the flag, on every watch — down
to the ones where each star is nine pixels across.

The time, and the date beneath it in wide letter-spaced capitals. Nothing else —
no battery meter, no step count. The empty field is the design.

On the black-and-white watches the band goes solid black and the composition
survives whole, because what carries it is the cut and where the emblem sits on
it, never the colour.
